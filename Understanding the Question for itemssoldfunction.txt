Understanding the Question

A salesperson earns:

A fixed salary of 200 per week.
Plus 9% commission on total sales.

The item values are:

Item	Price
1	239.99
2	129.75
3	99.95
4	350.89

The program should:

Ask which items the salesperson sold.
Add up the value of all items sold.
Calculate commission (9% of total sales).
Add 200 to the commission.
Display the earnings.
